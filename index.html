Services and Capabilities

Core Capabilities of PCB Manufacturing Services

  • Single-sided, double-sided, and multi-layer (up to 40+ layers)
  • Materials: FR4, Rogers, aluminum, polyimide (flex), ceramic, etc.
  • Board Types: Rigid, Flex, Rigid-Flex, HDI
  • Board Thickness: 0.2 mm to 3.2 mm (customizable)
  • Copper Weight: Standard 1 oz, 2 oz, up to 6+ oz
  • Surface Finishes: HASL, Lead Free HASL, ENIG, ENEPIG, OSP, Immersion Tin/Silver

PCB Assembly (PCBA)

  • Surface Mount Technology (SMT)
  • Through-Hole Technology (THT)
  • Mixed Technology (SMT + THT)

PCBA Services Typically Offered

  • Prototype and low-volume assembly
  • Turnkey assembly (full sourcing)
  • Consigned/kitted assembly
  • Box build and final product integration

Additional Support

  • Logistics coordination
  • NDA and IP protection
